Referral programme

You find the opportunity. We handle the business. You earn when it becomes a project.

  1. Find a building that may need scaffolding.
  2. Connect us with the builder, contractor or site engineer.
  3. We handle visit, measurement, quote, execution.
  4. Project confirmed and you become eligible.
  5. Earn the incentive agreed in advance.

Open to engineers, students, contractors, supervisors, suppliers and anyone with local construction contacts. No scaffolding knowledge needed. Incentives depend on project value, size, profitability and customer payment, and are agreed before a referral is accepted. No payout is guaranteed.

Is the estimator a quotation?

No. Final quantity needs a site assessment.

Can I take only material?

Yes, subject to availability.

How much will I earn as a referrer?

It is agreed per referral, in advance. Nothing is guaranteed.
