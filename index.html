What is cuplock scaffolding?

A modular, temporary structure that gives workers safe access to height. Vertical standards carry fixed cups, and ledgers lock into them quickly, which makes cuplock fast to erect and dismantle.

Where scaffolding is required

  • Plastering
  • Painting
  • Waterproofing
  • Facade work
  • Masonry
  • Maintenance
