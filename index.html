Understand scaffolding

What is cuplock scaffolding?

Scaffolding is a temporary, modular structure that gives workers safe access to height for building, plastering, painting, waterproofing and facade work. Cuplock is a popular system: vertical standards carry fixed cups, and horizontal ledgers lock into them in one quick action.

Standards

Vertical tubes with fixed cups at regular intervals.

Ledgers

Long and short horizontals that lock into the cups.

Braces

Diagonals that stiffen the structure.

Jacks

Base jacks and U-head jacks adjust height and support.

Where scaffolding is required

  • Plastering
  • Painting
  • Waterproofing
  • Facade work
  • Masonry
  • Maintenance
  • Industrial access
